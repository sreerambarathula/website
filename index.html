01 About

From sensing physical systems to designing what comes next.

I work at the intersection of thermal-fluid science, computational modelling, non-intrusive sensing, and physics-guided machine learning.

My published research uses acoustic and vibration measurements to identify boiling regimes, predict heat load, and reconstruct temperature fields. It brings together experiments, signal processing, inverse problems, and interpretable machine learning.

My current work extends that foundation toward simulation-driven design and control of cold plates, two-phase microchannels, and architected lattice structures—connecting high-fidelity physics to useful engineering decisions.

02 Current research

Four connected research directions

Reproducible workflows that move from simulation and measurement to robust design and control.

01

CFD · Digital twins

Advanced cold-plate design

Validated conjugate heat-transfer workflows for liquid-cooled cold plates, including hotspot sensitivity, hydraulic–thermal trade-offs, off-design robustness, uncertainty-aware surrogates, and thermo-mechanical constraints.

02

Lattices · Inverse design

Architected structures for CHT

Simulation-driven and graph-based design of lattice and TPMS structures for coupled flow, heat-transfer, and structural performance—including grading and topology comparison.

View open-source project ↗
03

Scientific ML · Operators

Physics-guided neural operators

Adaptive multi-basis neural-operator models for out-of-distribution transient heat conduction in heterogeneous composites, with emphasis on physical consistency, uncertainty, and generalization.

04

Two-phase flow · Control

Microchannel cooling & control

Validated two-phase CFD connected to reduced-order modelling and energy-aware coolant-flow control for dynamic, spatially nonuniform chip heat loads.
